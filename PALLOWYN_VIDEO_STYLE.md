# Pallowyn - Halloween / Spooky Seasonal Video Style Lock

Sister pipeline to dark-fantasy, cyberpunk, and horror (creepvale) niches --
same architecture (Higgsfield still -> Minimax Hailuo 2.3 animate -> ffmpeg
music mux -> Buffer schedule) but a distinct visual identity.

## Tone: cozy-spooky, NOT horror

This is the crucial distinction from creepvale (the separate Horror/Spooky
niche). Pallowyn is festive and whimsical Halloween imagery -- the kind of
spooky you'd see on a greeting card or a well-decorated front porch, not
genuine horror or dread. Think trick-or-treat, not nightmare fuel.

- Jack-o'-lanterns, candlelight, autumn leaves, misty October nights
- Witches, black cats, bats, cobwebs -- all in a warm/inviting way
- Haunted houses shown as charming and atmospheric, not threatening
- NO gore, NO jump-scare framing, NO genuinely disturbing imagery
- NO on-screen text, no captions (silent visual page, same as dark-fantasy)

## Visual rules

- Wide establishing shots, not close-up portraits
- Camera always locked static -- only ambient elements move (candle
  flicker, drifting fog, falling leaves, swaying branches)
- Palette: warm oranges and golds against deep purple-blue night skies
- 9:16 vertical, 1080p stills, 6s animated clips (Minimax Hailuo 2.3)
- Roughly half the scene bank has people (silhouettes/figures in costume,
  trick-or-treaters, a lone witch), half is pure environment/object shots

## Prompt templates

Still image prompt pattern:
"[scene description], warm jack-o'-lantern candlelight, deep purple-blue
autumn night sky, drifting mist, fallen leaves, whimsical dark-fantasy
illustration style, wide cinematic composition, 9:16 vertical, highly
detailed, no text, no watermark"

Animate prompt pattern:
"Camera completely locked and static, only [ambient element -- candle
flicker / drifting fog / falling leaves / swaying branches] moves gently,
cozy spooky Halloween atmosphere, subtle ambient motion only, no camera
movement, no zoom, no pan"

## Music

Suno-generated instrumental Halloween theme ("Carnival of the Macabre") --
fast, driving, playful/mischievous rather than pure-dread horror score.
